Before anyone quotes anything

How it starts

STEP 01

A short call

What you want done, where the property is, when you would like it, and what you are planning to spend. You will not get a price on that call, because nobody can price a yard they have not measured.

STEP 02

A walk of the site

Someone comes out and walks the property with you, looking at access, grade, drainage and what is already growing there. The first visit is free, and booking it commits you to nothing.

STEP 03

A concept and a range

When it makes sense, a short first design phase: a written concept and a planning range you can make decisions against. That phase is paid, and you see the fee before it starts.
