What a visit includes

Every visit

  • Mowing and edging where there is lawn
  • Hard surfaces cleaned down
  • Beds weeded
  • Light seasonal pruning and shaping
  • Green waste from that visit hauled away
  • A look at the irrigation as we go

Quoted separately, always approved first

  • Renovation and structural pruning
  • Tree work needing a climb, a lift or a chipper
  • Removing, replacing or adding plants
  • Lawn renovation, seed, sod, aeration
  • Irrigation beyond a quick fix at a head or emitter
  • Mulch, gravel and other materials we bring

Your agreement

Your service agreement is a separate document from any construction contract. These are the terms on the face of it.
Term What it says
Length Month to month. You are not locked in.
Ending it Either side, 30 days' notice in writing. No penalty, no term to buy out.
Cadence Weekly. A change to cadence changes the agreement, confirmed in writing.
Service day Thursday, by route. A day, not an arrival time.
Price review Once a year, printed on the face of the agreement. Next: April 2027.
Weather Most work goes ahead in light rain. When conditions make it damaging we move the visit inside the same week and tell you.

Prototype notes

  • Concept 1 of 3. The organising idea is the record: a maintenance account is a running obligation, so the portal is the log of what was done, what was noticed and what is waiting on the client. Paper first, green used only for marks and rules.
  • The tree badge is a stand-in. The real GMZ mark is still needed as SVG, per DESIGN-HANDOFF.md.
  • Photo slots are labelled plates, not photographs, following the same convention as the project placeholders.
  • No dollar figures. What a client pays per visit belongs in their agreement and in the estimating system, so billing here shows reference and state only.
  • Every policy sentence traces to an FAQ already marked published. Nothing states a term that has not been decided.
  • Accessibility kept: skip link, visible focus rings, aria-current on the active tab, one h1, alt text on every plate, tabular figures, and a reduced-motion block.
  • Client name, dates and notes are invented. Sample phone numbers would use the 555 range.
