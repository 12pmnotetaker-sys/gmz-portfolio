Why a phone first

The moment a client uses this is the morning of their service day, standing in the kitchen. Designing for the desk and squeezing it down gets that moment wrong. The same layout widens to two columns on a laptop, next visit and decisions on the left, visit history on the right.

State is a shape, not a colour

Anything waiting on a client carries a fill, a border and a label together, so it still reads as urgent to someone who cannot separate amber from green. Brand green stays on GMZ, not on status.

The upsell is the honesty

Every extra is a quote a client approves before it happens. That turns the part of maintenance clients dread, work appearing on an invoice, into the part they trust.

Prototype notes

  • Concept 2 of 3. Three screens shown in frames: home, one visit, decisions. Sans first for the interface, the serif kept for dates and headings so the brand voice still reads.
  • The tree badge is a stand-in until the real GMZ mark exists as SVG.
  • Photo slots are labelled plates, not photographs.
  • No dollar figures. Approving a quote refers to the quote, which is where the price lives.
  • Every policy sentence traces to an FAQ already marked published. Nothing invents a term.
  • Accessibility kept: skip link, visible focus rings, aria-current in each screen's tab bar, one h1, alt text on every plate, and a reduced-motion block.
  • Client name, dates and notes are invented sample data.
