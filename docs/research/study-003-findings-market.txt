### pw-landscape-maintenance-classification  [T1 / high]
CLAIM: California maintains a distinct prevailing wage classification,
  "Landscape Maintenance Laborer," which by its own terms "Applies Only to
  Routine Landscape Maintenance Work, Not New Landscape Construction." In
  determination NC-LML-2026-1 the basic hourly rate is $16.90 in every
  Northern California county listed, which is the California state minimum
  wage. Total hourly rates including employer payments are $17.59 in San Mateo
  County (routine work), $17.24 in Santa Clara County and $17.24 in San
  Francisco. Overtime is $26.04, $25.69 and $25.69 respectively.
WHY IT MATTERS: The statutory wage floor for public agency landscape
  maintenance sits roughly 24 percent BELOW the OEWS median wage for
  landscaping workers in this metro ($23.10/hr, May 2025, recorded in Study
  002). Public agency maintenance work is therefore NOT inflated by prevailing
  wage, as would be assumed by analogy with construction. A contractor serving
  public agencies may lawfully staff routine maintenance at a labour cost below
  the open market rate. Municipal maintenance pricing is consequently a
  conservative comparable, not a generous one.
CAVEAT: This applies to routine maintenance only. New landscape CONSTRUCTION
  for a public agency falls under the ordinary construction craft
  determinations, which are multiples of this rate. Municipal maintenance data
  and municipal construction data are therefore skewed in opposite directions
  and must never be compared with each other without separate adjustment.
SOURCE: California Department of Industrial Relations, General Prevailing Wage
  Determination NC-LML-2026-1, Landscape Maintenance Laborer, Northern California
URL: https://www.dir.ca.gov/OPRL/2026-1/PWD/Determinations/Northern/NC-LML.pdf
PUBLISHED: issued 2026-02-22, nominal expiration 2026-03-31, effective until
  superseded   SAMPLE: statewide determination   GEO: Northern California counties

### losgatos-bid-tab-2022  [T1 / high]
CLAIM: The Town of Los Gatos received two responsive bids for its landscape
  maintenance RFP, opened 2 June 2022, on identical scope:
      BrightView Landscape Services      $167,402.00
      Monarch Landscape Companies        $236,849.10
  The winning bid was 29.3 percent below the only other bid. Allocation across
  the four bid tables differed sharply between the two bidders: on Table A
  (Medians and Facilities) Monarch bid $26,921.70 against BrightView's
  $61,770.00, while on Table C (Mowing) Monarch bid $119,206.39 against
  BrightView's $67,056.00.
WHY IT MATTERS: Dispersion between two bidders on identical scope is large, and
  the allocation across line items is inconsistent between them. Individual line
  items are therefore not comparable across bidders; only contract totals and
  derived blended rates are.
SOURCE: Town of Los Gatos, Attachment 2, Landscape Maintenance RFP Bid Proposal
  Results, 6/2/2022 (public record, town document portal)
URL: https://weblink.losgatosca.gov/WebLink/0/edoc/1744963/x.pdf
PUBLISHED: 2022-06-02   SAMPLE: two bids, one solicitation   GEO: Los Gatos, CA
  (Santa Clara County)

### losgatos-blended-rate-2022  [T1 / high]
CLAIM: BrightView's winning bid priced every line item at a near-uniform blended
  rate of approximately $45.70 per man-hour, all-in. The bid price sheet carries
  a "Total Hours" column against each priced item, allowing the rate to be
  derived directly. Across all 21 priced line items the total is $167,402 over
  3,664 man-hours, a blended $45.69 per man-hour. Individual items range only
  from $44.67 to $47.38 per man-hour.
  The Town's bid price sheet states that unit prices "include all labor,
  equipment, fees of any kind, overhead, insurance, fuel, materials, surcharges,
  disposal fees, and any other costs," so this rate is the client's total price,
  not a labour cost.
WHY IT MATTERS: This is a directly observed, all-in market price per man-hour for
  routine landscape maintenance performed by a national contractor in Santa Clara
  County. It is the unit that a per-crew-day or per-truck-day figure can be
  compared against.
INFERENCE, NOT MEASURED: Monarch's man-hour estimates were not published. If
  Monarch assumed comparable hours, its bid implies roughly $64.64 per man-hour.
  Treat as an inference about the losing bid, not a finding.
SOURCE: Town of Los Gatos, Attachment 1 (Agreement) and BrightView Landscape
  Services Business Proposal of 27 May 2022, Attachment D Bid Price Sheet
URL: https://weblink.losgatosca.gov/WebLink/0/edoc/1744965/x.pdf
PUBLISHED: 2022-05-27   SAMPLE: one winning bid, 21 priced line items
  GEO: Los Gatos, CA (Santa Clara County)

### losgatos-extra-work-rates-2022  [T1 / medium]
CLAIM: The same bid price sheet carries separate labour rates for emergency and
  after-hours work, which the Town required to "reflect prevailing wages," across
  four categories (Foreman, Flagger, Laborer, Operator). The straight-time rates
  bid were $48.60, $55, $105 and $115, each with an overtime rate at exactly 1.5x
  ($73, $82.50, $157.50 and $175).
  Unit prices for incidental work were also bid: turf removal and replacement at
  $3.45 per square foot ($51,750 based on 15,000 sq ft), and PVC repairs and valve
  replacements at $300, $450 and $550 each.
CONFIDENCE NOTE: The mapping of each straight-time rate to its labour category
  could not be read reliably from the PDF's text layer; the four rate pairs are
  certain, the category assignment is not. Recorded as a set rather than a mapping.
WHY IT MATTERS: Extra and after-hours work is priced well above the $45.70
  blended contract rate, in some categories more than double it. Contracted
  routine work and call-out work are priced as different products.
SOURCE: BrightView Landscape Services Business Proposal of 27 May 2022,
  Attachment D Bid Price Sheet
URL: https://weblink.losgatosca.gov/WebLink/0/edoc/1744965/x.pdf
PUBLISHED: 2022-05-27   SAMPLE: one bidder   GEO: Los Gatos, CA (Santa Clara County)

### losgatos-contract-terms-2022  [T1 / high]
CLAIM: The executed agreement runs 1 July 2022 through 30 June 2027. Preventative
  maintenance is capped at $167,402 annually and unanticipated maintenance at
  $8,370 annually, for a total not to exceed $175,772 annually and a five-year
  base of $878,860 before CPI adjustment. Compensation in later years adjusts
  upward annually for CPI. The contractor pays the Town $100 per location per
  scheduled service in liquidated damages for every missed service, in addition
  to the monthly payment being reduced by the cost of that service.
WHY IT MATTERS: The liquidated damages figure is an independent signal of what
  the buyer considers a single visit to be worth, and the CPI ratchet is a
  working precedent for escalation language.
SOURCE: Town of Los Gatos, Agreement for Services with BrightView Landscape
  Services, Inc.
URL: https://weblink.losgatosca.gov/WebLink/0/edoc/1744962/Attachment%201%20-%20Agreement%20for%20Services%20-%20Brightview%20Landscape%20Services%202022.pdf
PUBLISHED: 2022-06-21 (dated for identification)   SAMPLE: one executed contract
  GEO: Los Gatos, CA (Santa Clara County)

### fairfield-contract-value-2022  [T1 / medium]
CLAIM: The City of Fairfield's landscape maintenance agreement with New Image
  Landscape Company for Landscaping and Lighting Maintenance District No. 13
  (North Cordelia) is capped at $313,804 annually, or $627,608 across the initial
  24-month term beginning 1 January 2022. The agreement carries a detailed
  frequency specification: mow weekly, edge weekly, spot weed control weekly,
  leaf and litter removal weekly on lawns and biweekly on shrub, tree and ground
  cover areas, complete irrigation system checks monthly, irrigation repairs
  within 24 working hours, shrub pruning three times per year (April, July,
  October), pond inspection twice per week. Unsatisfactory maintenance not
  corrected on re-inspection carries a $75 deduction from the month's billing.
WHY IT MATTERS: Useful as a scope-of-work benchmark for what a public agency
  specifies and enforces. Its price is NOT comparable per unit: the agreement
  states no acreage and no site count, so no denominator can be derived.
GEO CAVEAT: Solano County, not the Peninsula. Weaker geographic comparability
  than the Los Gatos record.
SOURCE: City of Fairfield, Contractor Services Agreement with New Image Landscape
  Company, LLMD No. 13 North Cordelia (public agenda attachment)
URL: https://fairfield.novusagenda.com/agendapublic/AttachmentViewer.ashx?AttachmentID=6565&ItemID=4069
PUBLISHED: execution date line blank, initial term begins 2022-01-01
  SAMPLE: one executed contract   GEO: Fairfield, CA (Solano County)

GAPS RECORDED

G1. No Peninsula agency bid tabulation was retrieved. The Los Gatos record is
    Santa Clara County; San Mateo County agencies were searched without success
    in the time spent. The City of Palo Alto's 2018 award staff report was
    located but its document server returns HTTP 403 to automated retrieval, so
    it could not be read. Retrieval by hand or by public records request remains
    open.

G2. All price evidence in this study is commercial and municipal. No residential
    per-visit or per-hour price from a Peninsula competitor was obtained. The
    residential market remains unevidenced, consistent with Study 001 gaps 44 and
    45 which recorded that no producer price index and no Bay Area price index
    exist for landscape maintenance services.

G3. Only one bid tabulation was obtained, with two bidders. A single solicitation
    is not a market. The 29.3 percent spread between the two bids suggests
    dispersion is wide, which means more solicitations are needed before any
    central tendency should be asserted.

G4. Monarch Landscape Companies' man-hour estimates were not published, so its
    implied rate per man-hour cannot be measured, only inferred from an assumption
    about hours.

G5. Los Gatos figures are from 2022 and carry a CPI ratchet. They have not been
    escalated to 2026 in this file. Study 002 recorded the local index at +3.8
    percent for the twelve months to June 2026 and the national gardening and
    lawncare services index at +12.3 percent over a comparable window; which of
    those better approximates four years of drift on this contract is not
    established.

G6. Whether public agency maintenance scope is comparable to residential scope at
    all is unresolved. Municipal work here is medians, parks and mowing on large
    contiguous sites. Residential garden maintenance differs in task mix, site
    size and access.
