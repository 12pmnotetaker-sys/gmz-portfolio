GMZ RESEARCH KNOWLEDGE BASE
========================================================================

WHAT THIS FOLDER IS

A permanent, sourced evidence base for GMZ Landscaping. It exists so research
stops evaporating: anything learned here can be cited later by a person or by
one of GMZ's agents, with the source and its date attached.

Xavier owns this folder. It is not in a sandbox and it does not disappear.

========================================================================
HOW TO USE IT

Each study gets its own set of files:

  <study> - Report      Readable. Executive summary, themes, decisions tested.
  <study> - Findings    Structured. One block per finding, for agents to parse.
  <study> - Gaps        What could not be established, and why that matters.

Read the Report. Search the Findings. Check the Gaps before asserting anything
the Findings do not cover.

========================================================================
THE RULES THAT KEEP THIS USEFUL

1. Every finding carries a source, a URL, a tier and a date. No exceptions.
   A claim without a traceable source does not belong in here.

2. Source tiers, and they are not interchangeable:
     T1  peer-reviewed, university extension, government statistics, statute
     T2  association research that discloses its sample size and method
     T3  trade press and software vendor surveys with disclosed methodology
   Rejected outright: marketing pages, contractor blogs, SEO articles, and any
   survey that will not say who was asked.

3. A T3 finding never moves a decision on its own.

4. Findings age. A benchmark from five years ago is evidence about five years
   ago. Statute and case law age differently: check for amendment.

5. Gaps are content. When the evidence is not there, that is worth recording,
   because the alternative is somebody guessing and it reading like a fact.

6. Nothing internal to GMZ goes in here. Costs, margins, rates, crew counts and
   client names live in the estimating system and in Xavier's facts sheet. This
   folder is about the outside world.
