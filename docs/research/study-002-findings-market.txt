  minimum deposit if environmental assessment required; permit void if
  unused within one year

  | The tree, located anywhere on the lot, smaller than 48" in
  | circumference, when measured 48" above grade [may be removed without a
  | permit] ... All other trees cannot be removed unless a permit has been
  | issued to the property owner or tree professional. ... Non-refundable
  | $750.00 application fee.
SOURCE: Town of Atherton, Tree Removal Procedure (official town handout, DocumentCenter)
URL: https://www.athertonca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/502/Tree-Removal-Procedure
PUBLISHED: unknown (undated town handout implementing AMC Chapter 8.10)   SAMPLE: n/a (regulation/procedure)   GEO: Atherton, CA
BEARS ON: Schedule and contingency on Atherton design/build jobs: one

  before removal or before pruning exceeding 25 percent of the canopy, with
  required arborist reports prepared by a consulting arborist from the
  city's approved list.

  (15 in. dia.); measured at 54 in. above natural grade; permit at >25%

  | All trees other than oaks which have a trunk with a circumference of
  | 47.1 inches (diameter of fifteen (15) inches) or more, measured fifty-
  | four (54) inches above natural grade. ... An oak tree (Quercus) which is
  | native to California and has a trunk with a circumference of 31.4 inches
  | (diameter of ten (10) inches) or more, measured at fifty-four (54)
  | inches above natural grade.
SOURCE: City of Menlo Park, city news 'Preserve or remove? The Heritage Tree Ordinance' (quoting Municipal Code Ch. 13.24)
URL: https://www.menlopark.gov/News-articles/City-news/20230327-Preserve-or-remove-The-Heritage-Tree-Ordinance-and-emergency-removal-permits

### far-6-percent-design-production-cap  [T1 / high]
CLAIM: Federal civilian procurement caps the fee for production and delivery
  of design documents at 6 percent of estimated construction cost, and the
  cap applies only to that narrow production slice of A/E services, not to
  the full scope (studies, preliminary engineering, construction-phase
  support are outside the cap).
FIGURE: 6 percent of estimated construction cost
QUOTE (verbatim):
  | For architect-engineer services for public works or utilities, the
  | contract price or the estimated cost and fee for production and delivery
  | of designs, plans, drawings, and specifications shall not exceed 6
  | percent of the estimated cost of construction of the public work or
  | utility, excluding fees.
SOURCE: FAR 15.404-4(c)(4)(i)(B), Acquisition.gov (implementing 10 U.S.C. 3322(b) and 41 U.S.C. 3905)
URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/15.404-4
PUBLISHED: current FAR text as fetched 2026-08-15   SAMPLE: n/a (regulation)   GEO: United States (federal civilian contracts)
BEARS ON: The design-agreement fee percentage and the FAQ answer about
  design fees: the oft-quoted federal 6 percent is a cap on drawings-
  production only, on public works vastly larger than a Peninsula residence,
  so it is not a comparable ceiling for GMZ's full design service.
CAVEAT: Applies to public works or utilities at federal scale; the capped
  scope is much narrower than a residential design engagement that includes
  site analysis, client meetings, MWELO documentation and permit support.
  Misreading 6 percent as 'what design costs' understates every published
