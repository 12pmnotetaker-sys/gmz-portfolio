# Research working files

The knowledge base itself lives in Google Drive, in **GMZ Research Knowledge Base**,
which Xavier owns. That is the home; this folder is the working copy from the
session that produced Study 001, kept so the raw output is not lost to an
ephemeral container.

- `report.txt` — readable report (uploaded to Drive)
- `findings-T1.txt`, `findings-T2-T3.txt` — the 71 verified findings with sources
- `gaps.txt` — the 75 questions no tiered source could answer
- `study-001-raw.json` — the unprocessed workflow output
- `study-002-report.txt` — Study 002: legal primary texts, the Peninsula local
  layer, fee anchors, escalator numbers, acquisition economics
- `study-002-findings-legal.txt`, `study-002-findings-market.txt` — 66 findings,
  43 carrying verbatim statute or regulation text
- `study-002-gaps.txt` — 51 gaps
- `study-002-counsel-brief.txt` — one-visit brief for a California construction
  attorney: five questions with the assembled primary texts
- `study-002-escalator-design.txt` — maintenance renewal and escalator design,
  with the real index numbers
- `study-002-rebate-reference.txt` — per-town turf-replacement rebate reference
- `study-002-raw.json` — the unprocessed workflow output
- `study-003-findings-market.txt` — Study 003, in progress: public agency
  landscape maintenance pricing. 7 findings and 6 gaps, including a derived
  all-in rate per man-hour from a public bid price sheet, and the prevailing
  wage determination that governs routine landscape maintenance
- `README.txt` — the knowledge base rules (uploaded to Drive)

Study 003 covers Workstream 1 of the pricing research plan only. It has no

findings file until the study is finished.
