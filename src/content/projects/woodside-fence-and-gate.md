The existing fence had been repaired in sections over twenty years and had
drifted off the line by nearly a foot at the far corner. Rebuilding it meant
starting from the survey, not from the old posts.

## What we built

Posts are set in concrete footings dug below the frost-free depth and to the
diameter the span calls for, spaced evenly rather than to the old holes. The
fence itself is a good-neighbor pattern so it reads the same from both sides.
A pair of driveway gates hangs on hardware sized for the span, on posts a size
up from the run.

## Notes

The grade falls almost three feet across the run. The fence steps with it in
even increments rather than raking, which keeps every panel square and the top
line legible from the road.
