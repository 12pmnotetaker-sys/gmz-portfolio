The front lawn was drinking more water than the rest of the property combined
and still going brown at the edges. The owners wanted it gone, and they wanted
what replaced it to look intentional from the street.

## What we built

A decomposed-granite walkway runs from the drive to the porch on a compacted,
stabilized base, so it holds a clean edge and sheds water instead of channeling
it. Either side is planted in layers: low grasses and perennials at the front,
shrubs behind them, and two multi-trunk trees set to frame the entry without
closing it in.

Everything is on drip, split into zones by water need rather than by bed, and
run by a smart controller that adjusts to the weather. Path lighting is set low
along the walkway.

## Notes

The old lawn's sprinkler heads were capped rather than pulled, and the mainline
was left in place and pressure-tested. If the owners ever want a small lawn
panel back, the water is already there.
