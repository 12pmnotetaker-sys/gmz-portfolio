Maintenance is not the same product as a build, and it is not priced or
scheduled the same way. This account runs weekly, on a fixed day, with a
written schedule the owner has a copy of.

## What the visit covers

Mowing and edging, blowing down hard surfaces, bed weeding, and pruning on the
schedule the plants want rather than the schedule the calendar wants. Irrigation
gets walked zone by zone once a month, with the controller checked against the
season.

## Notes

A standing account means the same crew sees the same garden every week. Problems
get caught small: a head knocked out of alignment, a valve weeping, a branch
rubbing a roofline.
