The side yard had been a pass-through: a strip of failing lawn between the
house and the fence line, wet through the winter and bare by August. The
brief was to make it somewhere to sit.

## What we built

A stone terrace sets the floor and holds the grade against the house. A low
seat wall wraps the north edge, giving the space a back and doubling the
seating without furniture. Beds run the length of the fence, planted for
structure first and color second, so the space reads as finished in every
season rather than only in spring.

Irrigation runs as three zones: beds on drip, the two specimen trees on their
own line, and the containers on a short cycle off a smart controller. Lighting
is low and warm, set into the wall rather than staked into the beds.

## Notes

Drainage drove the sequence. The yard was regraded and a line run to daylight
before a single stone was set, because a terrace built over standing water is
a terrace you rebuild.
