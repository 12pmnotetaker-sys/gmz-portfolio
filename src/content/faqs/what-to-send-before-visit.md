Photos and a few plain facts, sent ahead, make the visit much more useful. Send photos of the whole area from several angles, including the parts you are unhappy with, plus anything you already have: a plot plan, drawings from an architect or designer, HOA rules that apply to your yard, and pictures of yards you like.

Two details change the work more than people expect: how a truck and a small machine would reach the work area, including the narrowest gate, and whether water collects anywhere after rain. For a maintenance account it is simpler: photos, whether there is irrigation, and which day suits you. None of it is required.
