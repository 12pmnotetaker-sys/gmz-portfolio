Photos and a few plain facts, sent ahead, make the visit much more useful. Send photos of the whole area from several angles, including the parts you are unhappy with, and anything you have already collected: a plot plan or survey, drawings from an architect or designer, HOA rules that apply to your yard, and pictures of yards you like.

Two details are worth writing down because they change the work more than people expect. First, how a truck and a small machine would reach the work area, and how wide the narrowest gate is. Second, whether water collects anywhere after rain. Also useful: what you want kept, and what you have already decided versus what is still open.

For a maintenance account it is simpler. Photos of the property, whether there is an irrigation system and roughly how it is set up, and which day of the week suits you. None of this is required. If you would rather just talk, call and bring the rest to the visit.
