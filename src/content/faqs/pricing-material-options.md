Yes, and on the big items we would rather you asked. Tell us which two levels you want to see and we price the identical scope both ways, so the only thing that changes between the two documents is the material.

We do this because the material level is the largest single lever in a landscape price, and there is no honest midpoint to guess at. Pricing one tier and calling it the answer hides the decision from you. Seeing the same patio priced two ways puts the trade in front of you in dollars, which is where it belongs. We will also tell you where we think the money works hardest: a smaller area in the better material usually reads better than a large area in the cheaper one, and there are places where nobody will ever notice the difference.

The same applies to phasing. If the whole scope is more than you want to commit to now, we can price it in stages, so the parts that only happen once go in together: base, drainage and irrigation lines. The visible surfaces follow later. Tell us at bid time, because phasing decided after the crew has left costs more than phasing designed in.
