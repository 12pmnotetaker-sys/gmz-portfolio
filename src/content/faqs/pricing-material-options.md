Yes, and on the big items we would rather you asked. Tell us which two levels you want to see and we price the identical scope both ways, so the only thing that changes between the two documents is the material.

Material level is the largest single lever in a landscape price, and there is no honest midpoint to guess at. Seeing the same patio priced two ways puts the trade in front of you in dollars, which is where it belongs. We will also tell you where the money works hardest: a smaller area in the better material usually reads better than a large area in the cheaper one.
