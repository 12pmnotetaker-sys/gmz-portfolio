You say so, and we revise it. Nobody gets a yard right on the first try, and the first presentation exists to be reacted to. We would far rather hear that a plan is wrong on paper than watch it get built.

Two rounds of revision are included in the design fee. Anything beyond that is priced in writing before we start on it, so there is never a surprise on the invoice. There is a distinction worth naming: moving a patio, changing a plant palette or reworking a bed line is a revision. Adding a whole new area of the property, or changing what the project is, is new scope, and it moves both the design and the construction number.

Your design agreement says all of this on its face. Read it before you sign, and ask us about anything in it that is not clear.
