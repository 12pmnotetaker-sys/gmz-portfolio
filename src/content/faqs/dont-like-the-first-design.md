You say so, and we revise it. Nobody gets a yard right on the first try, and the first presentation exists to be reacted to. We would far rather hear that a plan is wrong on paper than watch it get built.

How many rounds of revision are included is being settled now. What we recommend, and what we would write, is two rounds included in the design fee, with anything beyond that priced in writing before we start on it. There is a distinction worth naming: moving a patio, changing a plant palette or reworking a bed line is a revision. Adding a whole new area of the property, or changing what the project is, is new scope, and it moves both the design and the construction number.

Ask what your agreement includes before you sign it, and get the answer in the document rather than in conversation.
