Because a landscape price is built from a measured site and chosen materials, and until both exist an honest number is a range. A firm-looking number this early is a guess wearing a suit.

The range tightens as the property is measured and the materials are settled. What you get first is a planning budget, wide enough to be honest and narrow enough to decide with.
