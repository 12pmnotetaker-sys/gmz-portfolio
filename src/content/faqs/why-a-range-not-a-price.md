Because nobody has measured your property yet, and a single number before that would be a guess dressed up as a commitment. The first number you get from GMZ is a range. It is built from a walk of the site, photos and rough dimensions, not from a measured survey and a final materials list. Those two things move a landscape price more than anything else, and neither is settled at this point.

Here is how much they move it. The surface material you choose can move the biggest part of the price a long way, and the same patio in a different material is a different project financially. What sits under the existing surface, and whether a truck can reach the back of the property, changes the labor before a single thing is installed. We can tell you the honest window your project sits in today. We cannot hand you a fixed price for work that has not been drawn or measured, and a number given before that work is done usually turns into a change order later.

The number tightens as the site is measured and the materials are chosen. Measuring costs you nothing.
