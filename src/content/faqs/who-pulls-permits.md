GMZ files the permit and manages the inspections as the licensed contractor of record. The fee is billed to you as a separate line at whatever the town charges, not folded into the price.

Most residential landscape work on the Peninsula needs no permit. What commonly does: a retaining wall over a certain height, structures you can stand under, electrical, gas lines, anything that changes how water leaves your property, and on larger projects the state's landscape water-efficiency review. Every town sets its own thresholds and timeline, so we tell you at bid time which we expect to apply and what it does to your start date.
