GMZ works the San Francisco Peninsula and is based in Redwood City. The towns we work in regularly are Redwood City, Atherton, Menlo Park, Palo Alto, Woodside, San Carlos, Belmont, Portola Valley, Los Altos and Hillsborough.

If you are near that list but not on it, ask. For a build project a little extra distance is usually workable, because the crew parks once in the morning and works the same ground all day. For maintenance it matters more: maintenance runs on fixed-day routes, so a property well away from an existing route may not be one we can serve properly. We would rather tell you that than take the account and become the contractor who keeps arriving late.
