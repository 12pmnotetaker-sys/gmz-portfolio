Usually, yes. A route is worked by the same team week after week, and that is deliberate. They learn where the valve box is, which shrub the dog gets behind, which corner holds water, and how you like the beds edged. A property served by whoever happened to be free that morning gets maintained to the average of everyone who has ever been there.

We will not promise the identical people on every single visit, because people take vacation, get sick and sometimes move on, and that is a promise we would end up breaking. When somebody covers, they work from the record for your property: the photos and notes from the first assessment visit, and what has been done since. A covering team should not arrive blind.

If a visit is ever done poorly, tell us that week rather than months later. It is easy to put right while everyone still remembers the day.
