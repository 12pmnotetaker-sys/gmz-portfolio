Not a number, no. A price given by someone who has not seen your property is either useless or misleading, and it tends to become the figure you remember for the rest of the project. We would rather not start you there. We also do not publish a starting-at figure, for the same reason: it would be a number attached to nothing.

What a phone call is good for is deciding quickly whether we are a fit. If what you are describing and what you want to spend are not in the same neighborhood, we will say so on the call rather than after the drive. If they are, we book a visit, and that is where the useful conversation actually happens. For a design or build project the visit carries a fee, and you hear what it is on the call before anything is booked.

If you are asking about maintenance rather than a build, the answer is the same for the same reason: we walk the property first, and that walk carries a fee of its own, quoted on the call. Maintenance is priced separately from construction work and runs under its own service agreement, with its own schedule and its own terms.
