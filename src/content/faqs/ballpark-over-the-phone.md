Not honestly, no. A price without a measured site and chosen materials is a guess, and a guess that turns out low is worse for you than no number at all.

What a phone call is good for is deciding quickly whether we are a fit. Tell us the scope and roughly what you want to spend, and we will say on the call if those are not in the same neighborhood, rather than after the drive. If they are, we book the visit.
