Yes. GMZ Landscaping Inc. is a licensed California landscape contractor, and our CSLB license number is published on this site so you can check it yourself. Look it up on the Contractors State License Board website, which shows the license status, the classification, and the bond and workers' compensation records on file. It is free, it takes about a minute, and it is worth doing for every contractor you are considering, not only this one.

If you need a certificate of insurance for an HOA, a property manager or your own file, ask and one will be sent to you. We work out of a P.O. Box rather than a storefront, which is normal for a landscape contractor; the license number is the thing to verify us by.
