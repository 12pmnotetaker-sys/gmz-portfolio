Yes, you get a fixed day of the week and we keep it. Routes are built by area so the crew is working rather than driving, which means your day is not very moveable once it is set. If you need a particular day, say so before we start and we will tell you what the route allows.

We give you a day, not an arrival time. The stops ahead of you vary, so the hour moves. You do not need to be home. What we do need is reliable access on your day: a gate code or a key if there is a lock, dogs secured, and a working outdoor spigot.

Weather and holidays are the two exceptions. Most maintenance work goes ahead in light rain. When conditions make the work pointless or damaging, we move the visit within the same week where the route allows it, and we tell you rather than leaving you to notice.
