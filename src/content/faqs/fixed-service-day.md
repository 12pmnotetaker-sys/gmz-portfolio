Yes, you get a fixed day of the week and we keep it. Routes are built by area so the crew is working rather than driving, which means your day is not very moveable once it is set. If you need a particular day, say so before we start.

We give you a day, not an arrival time, because the stops ahead of you vary. You do not need to be home, but we do need reliable access: a gate code or a key, dogs secured, and a working outdoor spigot. When weather makes the work pointless or damaging we move the visit within the same week where the route allows, and we tell you rather than leaving you to notice.
