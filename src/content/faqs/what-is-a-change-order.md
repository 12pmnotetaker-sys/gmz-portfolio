A written amendment to the contract, priced and signed by both of us before the work happens, covering anything outside the original scope. Nothing reaches your bill without one. If someone on site says a change is no problem and it never appears in writing, that is not generosity; it is a bill you have not seen yet.

There are three causes and two are avoidable: scope you add mid-job, decisions made late, and genuine unknowns underground. Keep the count near zero by choosing materials before we start, reading the exclusions page, and making your while-you-are-here list before the contract rather than in week two.
