Anything that changes the garden rather than keeping it as it is. In: mowing, edging, blowing down hard surfaces, weeding, seasonal pruning, hauling that visit's green waste, and checking the irrigation as we go.

Out: tree work needing a climb or a lift, renovation pruning, planting and plant removal, lawn renovation, irrigation repair beyond a head or an emitter, fertilizer and treatment programs, storm and heavy leaf cleanup, mulch and gravel top-ups, hard surface and fence repair, pet waste, and holiday lighting. None of that is a refusal. Most of it we can take on, priced and approved separately so it never lands on an invoice unannounced.
