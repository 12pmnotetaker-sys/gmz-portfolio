Anything that changes the garden rather than keeping it as it is. Routine care is in: mowing, edging, blowing down hard surfaces, weeding, seasonal pruning of shrubs and perennials, hauling the green waste from that visit, and checking the irrigation as we go. What follows is out, and it is a long list on purpose.

Not included: tree work beyond what can be reached safely from the ground, and anything needing a climb, a lift or a chipper; structural or heavy renovation pruning; removing plants, replacing plants, and any new planting; seasonal color changeouts; lawn renovation, reseeding, sod, aeration and dethatching; irrigation repair beyond a quick fix at a head or an emitter, which means valves, wiring, mainline breaks, new zones and controller replacement are all separate; fertilizer, compost and soil amendment programs; pest, disease and weed treatments calling for a licensed applicator; storm cleanup and debris we did not generate; heavy leaf drop through the fall; mulch and gravel top-ups, which are a material purchase; pressure washing, gutters and roofs; hardscape, fence, deck, lighting and drainage repair; pet waste; moving furniture and planters; and holiday lighting.

None of that is a refusal. Much of it we can take on as separate work, and where a job calls for a licensed specialty trade we bring in the right one. Either way it is priced and approved separately, so nobody meets it on an invoice. Writing the line down where you can see it is easier for both of us than arguing about it later. The other half is what we need from you: access on your day, pets contained, water available, and any HOA rules that limit what can be cut or removed.
