Yes. A design or build consultation is $175 for the first hour on the property, paid when you book it. A maintenance property walk is $75. If you are outside the towns we serve, add $100 for travel, and we do not go much beyond forty miles.

None of it is credited against later work. What you get for it is an hour of a licensed contractor on your ground, saying what is possible and what it will take, which is worth having whether or not you build with us.
