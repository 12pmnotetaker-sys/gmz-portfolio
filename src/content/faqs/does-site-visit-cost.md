No. The first site visit is free. Someone comes out, walks the property with you, looks at access, grade, drainage and what is already growing there, and talks through what you want. Booking it commits you to nothing.

Two things are worth knowing before it happens. The visit is a working conversation rather than a sales call, and it is also where GMZ works out whether this is a project we are the right contractor for, so expect direct questions about scope, timing and what you are planning to spend. And a visit is not a bid. What comes out of it is a shared understanding of the work and, when it makes sense, an offer to start a short first design phase: a written concept and a planning range. That phase is paid, and you see the fee before it starts.
