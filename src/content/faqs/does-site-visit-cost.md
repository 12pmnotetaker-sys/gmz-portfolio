For a design or build project, yes: $175, which covers the first hour on the property. For a maintenance account the property walk is still free.

Someone comes out, walks the site with you, looks at access, grade, drainage and what is already growing there, and talks through what you want the space to be like. If it is going to need longer than the hour, you hear that while we are standing there rather than on an invoice afterwards.

Two things are worth knowing about the fee. It is paid when you book rather than collected at your door, so nothing has to be decided on the spot and nobody is chasing you for it later. And it is not credited against anything that follows. The visit, the design and the construction each carry their own price, for the same reason the design fee is not folded into the construction number: you get to see each cost on its own and judge it, instead of finding it buried inside a bigger one.

What the fee buys is an hour of undivided attention from someone who has done this a long time, and a straight answer. The visit is a working conversation rather than a sales call, and it is also where GMZ works out whether this is a project we are the right contractor for, so expect direct questions about scope, timing and what you are planning to spend. It is still not a bid. What comes out of it is a shared understanding of the work and, when it makes sense, an offer to start a short first design phase: a written concept and a planning range, priced on its own, with the fee shown before it starts.

If your property is outside the towns GMZ works in, there is a travel charge on top of the visit fee. Ask on the first call and you will hear both numbers before you agree to anything.
