Yes. Building to someone else's plans is one of the normal ways a project comes to us, alongside designing it ourselves. There is no design fee in that case, because we are not doing the design.

What we need from you is the full set: the plan, the plant list, and any grading, drainage or construction details that exist. It helps considerably if whoever drew it is reachable for questions. We walk the property and check the drawings against the ground before we price anything. Where a plan does not match the site, and that does happen, you hear about it then, not halfway through construction.

One line worth being clear on: the person who designed it carries the design, and we carry the build. If a detail will not work as drawn, we will say so and propose a fix rather than build something we think is wrong and leave you with it.
