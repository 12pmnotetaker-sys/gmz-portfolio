Yes. Building to someone else's plans is one of the normal ways a project comes to us, and there is no design fee in that case because we are not doing the design.

What we need is the full set: the plan, the plant list, and any grading, drainage or construction details that exist. It helps considerably if whoever drew it is reachable for questions. We walk the property and check the drawings against the ground before we price anything, so where a plan does not match the site, and that does happen, you hear about it then rather than halfway through construction. The designer carries the design and we carry the build.
