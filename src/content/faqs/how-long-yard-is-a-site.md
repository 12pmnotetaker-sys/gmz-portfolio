Longer than the working days on the schedule, and you should hear that now rather than in week three. A build is not continuous work: days of machines and noise, then a quiet stretch waiting on a cure, an inspection or a delivery. Your yard is out of use from the first day of demolition to the last day of cleanup.

The rough half comes first, demolition and grading, with part of your frontage staging material and debris. The back half is quieter and finer. You get a working schedule at the pre-construction meeting and an update on the same day each week.
