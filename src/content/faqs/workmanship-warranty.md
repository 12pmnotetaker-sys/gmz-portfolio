One year on workmanship from the date your project is complete is the term we work to, and we will put it in writing in your contract before you sign. It covers the work of our hands: paving that settles, a wall that moves, a drain that will not drain, a leaking irrigation fitting, a light that fails because of how it was wired. If it is our installation, we come back and put it right at no cost to you.

It does not cover plants, which we answer separately, and it does not cover things outside our control: another trade working the same ground after we leave, a change somebody else made, storm damage, or a root from a neighboring tree. Where materials and equipment carry a manufacturer's coverage, that coverage is yours and we will help you use it.

One thing to be straight about: the written warranty page is still being finalized, so do not rely on a verbal term from anybody, including us. Ask for the current warranty language, read it, and get it attached to your contract before work starts.
