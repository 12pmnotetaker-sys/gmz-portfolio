A short conversation, then a decision about whether a site visit makes sense for both of us. Expect to be asked what you want done, where the property is, when you would like it done, who else is part of the decision, and what you are planning to spend. That last question is asked as much for your benefit as ours: it is how you find out early whether GMZ is in the right range.

You will not get a price on that call. What you can get is whether this is work GMZ does, whether your property is in the area we serve, and a date to walk it.
