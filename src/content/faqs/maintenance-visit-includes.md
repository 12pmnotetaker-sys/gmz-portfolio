Routine care: the work that keeps a garden looking the way it did the week we finished it. Mowing and edging where there is lawn, cleaning down the hard surfaces, weeding the beds, light seasonal pruning of shrubs, perennials and grasses, and hauling away the green waste we generate that day.

We walk the irrigation while we are there. A head knocked out of line, a drip line pulled loose by a dog, a zone that quietly stopped running: easy to catch on a regular visit, painful to find after a dry month. We work from a standing list for your property, and anything falling outside the visit we tell you about rather than doing it and billing you after.
