A visit is routine care: the work that keeps a garden looking the way it did the week we finished it. On a typical residential property that means mowing and edging where there is lawn, cleaning down the hard surfaces, weeding the beds, light seasonal pruning and shaping of shrubs, perennials and grasses, and hauling away the green waste we generate that day.

We also walk the irrigation while we are there. A head knocked out of line, a drip line pulled loose by a dog, a zone that quietly stopped running: those are easy to catch on a regular visit and painful to find after a month of dry weather has taken the plants on that zone. We adjust controller run times as the seasons change, and we tell you what we saw.

We work from a standing list for your property rather than from whatever catches the eye that morning. If we see something that falls outside the visit, we tell you about it instead of doing it and billing you afterward.
