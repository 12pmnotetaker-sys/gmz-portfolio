Call the number on your paperwork, or any number on this site, and tell us what you are seeing. You will hear back within two business days, and sooner when water is running where it should not be.

Someone comes out and looks before anyone talks about causes or costs. Landscape problems are usually simple to diagnose in person and nearly impossible to diagnose over the phone. Once we have seen it, we tell you plainly what happened, what putting it right involves, and where the cost sits, before any work is done.

Keep whatever plans and settings came with your project handy; they make the visit faster.
