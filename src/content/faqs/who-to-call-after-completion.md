Call the office number on your paperwork and ask for the person who ran your job. That is the fastest route, because they know the ground, the zones and what is buried where. Please do not route it through a crew member's personal phone: work that is not written down at the office is work that gets lost.

Have three things ready when you call: what is failing, when it started, and a photo. For irrigation, the zone number if you know it. That is usually enough to tell whether it is a fitting, a valve, the controller or a plant that was never getting water, and it decides whether we bring a truck or talk you through it on the phone.

Then we come and look. If it is our workmanship, we put it right at no cost to you. If it turns out to be something else, a repair somebody else made, storm damage, or a plant that dried out, we will tell you plainly and give you a price to put it right rather than quietly adding it to a bill. Water against the house or a leak you cannot shut off should be called in the same day.
