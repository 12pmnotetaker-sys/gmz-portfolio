On how much your property takes to service properly, and how often we come. We walk the property before pricing anything: how much lawn there is, how much planted bed, how much pruning it will want across a year, how much green waste comes off it, and how easily equipment moves around it.

That produces one set price per visit, agreed before the first visit, which does not move from week to week because one visit ran longer than another. Two things sit outside it: work that is not part of routine care, and materials we bring for you. Both are priced and approved before we do them, never added to a bill after the fact.

The walk itself carries a fee, quoted on the call before it is booked, and it is smaller than the fee on a design or build consultation because the visit is shorter. It is not a free quote and we would rather say so plainly than have you find out when it is booked.

We do not price maintenance off a photo or a look from the street. A number produced that way is a number for a property nobody has seen, and it changes once someone finally walks it. That is the whole reason the walk exists, and it is why it is worth something.
