On how much your property takes to service properly, and how often we come. We walk it before pricing anything: how much lawn there is, how much planted bed, how much pruning it will want across a year, how much green waste comes off it, and how easily equipment moves around it.

That produces one set price per visit, agreed before the first visit, which does not move from week to week because one visit ran longer. Two things sit outside it: work that is not routine care, and materials we bring for you. Both are priced and approved before we do them. We do not price maintenance off a photo or a look from the street.
