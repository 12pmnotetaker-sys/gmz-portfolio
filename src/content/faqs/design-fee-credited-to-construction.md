No. The design fee is not credited toward construction, and it is not refunded when you build with us. We say that plainly and early because plenty of clients expect it to be credited, and contract signing is the worst possible moment to find out otherwise.

The reason is straightforward. Design and construction are two separate pieces of work, and each carries its own price. The design hours are spent before a shovel moves, and they are spent whether or not you go ahead. If the fee were credited, the construction price would simply have to carry those hours somewhere you could not see them. We would rather show you both numbers and let you judge each one on its own.

What the fee buys is a plan drawn from real measurements of your property, and a construction price built from that plan instead of from a guess.
