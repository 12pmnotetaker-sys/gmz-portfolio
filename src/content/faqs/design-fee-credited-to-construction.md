No. The design fee is not credited toward construction, and it is not refunded when you build with us. We say that plainly and early because plenty of clients expect it to be credited, and contract signing is the worst possible moment to find out otherwise.

Design and construction are two separate pieces of work, and each carries its own price. The design hours are spent before a shovel moves, whether or not you go ahead. If the fee were credited, the construction price would simply have to carry those hours somewhere you could not see them. We would rather show you both numbers and let you judge each one on its own.
