Yes, and it is common. Phasing is a real option rather than a consolation prize, and it works best when every phase is drawn on one plan at the start, so the property is not built twice.

The order matters more than the split does. Grading, drainage and anything that runs under a patio or walkway goes first, even when the visible part of that area is years away; sleeves for irrigation and lighting are inexpensive while the ground is open and costly once there is concrete over them. Each phase is its own agreement, priced when it happens rather than years in advance, and phasing costs somewhat more in total because setup and cleanup happen more than once.
