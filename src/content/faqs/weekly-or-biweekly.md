question: 'Should I be on a weekly or an every-other-week schedule?'

Weekly if you have lawn, a lot of planted bed, or you want the property to look tidy every day of the week. Every other week works well for gardens that are mostly established shrubs, groundcover or gravel, where growth is slow and there is not much to pick up.

The honest test is the growing season, not the winter. From spring into early summer a lawn on a two-week cycle looks shaggy well before the next visit, and the visit stops being maintenance and starts being catch-up. Plenty of properties want weekly through the growing months and settle into every other week after. Changing cadence changes your agreement and your price.
