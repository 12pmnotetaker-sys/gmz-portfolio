Yes, and it runs exactly as it would for anyone else: a paid site consultation, then a signed design agreement opening with a short first phase, a written concept and a planning range.

Being a maintenance client does not shortcut the design step and does not change how the work is priced. Measured drawings and a firm price come after the concept, the same as for a new client.
