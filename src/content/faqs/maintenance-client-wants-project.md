Yes, and it runs the same way it would for anyone else with one exception in your favour: there is no charge for the site consultation, because the crew is already on your property every week or two. After that it is the same path, a signed design agreement that opens with a short first phase, a written concept and a preliminary budget with a planning range. Measured drawings and a firm price come after that. Being a maintenance client does not shortcut the design step and does not change how the work is priced.

What it does change is how much we already know. We have been on your property every week or two, so we know the irrigation, the soil, where water sits after a wet winter and what has failed there before. That usually makes the concept sharper and the list of open questions shorter, and it takes some of the guesswork out of the budget range.

Your maintenance continues during the build. Where construction touches ground the crew normally services, we will tell you what happens to those visits for that stretch rather than leaving you to work it out from an invoice. The two agreements stay separate: the build is a construction contract, the maintenance is a service agreement, and neither absorbs the other.
