question: "Why aren't the plants guaranteed unless you're the ones maintaining the garden?"

Because once we drive away, the two things that decide whether a plant lives, water and care, are out of our hands. A plant is not a paver. It can be chosen well and planted well and still die of a controller switched off during a vacation, or a heat wave nobody adjusted for.

What changes with a maintenance account is control. If our crew is watering, adjusting the controller through the seasons and watching the beds every week, problems get caught while they are small. Either way, if something in the garden is failing, call us. A struggling plant is often really an irrigation problem.
