Often, yes. Where our routes allow it, a one-time cleanup or a short run of catch-up visits is work we take: overgrowth cut back, beds weeded and edged, the irrigation checked, the place brought back to presentable.

Route fit is the honest constraint. Our crews run fixed-day routes, so a one-off works best when your property sits near where a truck already is. Tell us your town and we will tell you what is possible that week. A lot of one-time cleanups turn into standing accounts once the hard reset is done, because keeping a garden presentable costs a fraction of rescuing it.
