Sometimes, and it depends on the schedule. Tell us what you are actually after, because a one-time cleanup is a different thing from maintenance: a defined job with a start and a finish, priced on the work in front of us rather than a recurring visit on a route.

Our position is that these are worth doing when they fit the week, and especially where a property is heading toward ongoing service or a build. A standalone cleanup dropped into a full week displaces work already promised to somebody else, so the answer is sometimes no, and that is better said early than late.

Whether we offer cleanups as a standing service is being settled. Send a few photos along with your town and we will tell you straight.
