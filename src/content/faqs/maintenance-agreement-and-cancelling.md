You are not locked into a long commitment, and our position is that you should be able to end it in writing with no penalty. There is a written service agreement, and it is a separate document from any construction contract you may have with us. It states the scope of a visit, the cadence, your day, the price, what is included, what is not, and how either side ends it. Maintenance is a standing obligation rather than a job with a finish line, so it needs terms a construction contract does not have.

We hold ourselves to the same notice in the other direction. An agreement should keep a schedule honest, not trap anybody. The exact notice period and the renewal terms are being finalized and will be printed on the face of the agreement, so you read them before you sign rather than after.

If something is not working, the better first move is usually a conversation. A cancellation is often a scope problem or a cadence problem that nobody raised while it was still easy to fix.
