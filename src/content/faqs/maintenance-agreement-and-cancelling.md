You are not locked in. The service agreement runs month to month and either side can end it with 30 days' notice in writing. There is no penalty and no term to buy out.

It is a separate document from any construction contract, and it states the scope of a visit, the cadence, your service day, the price, what is and is not included, and that the price is reviewed once a year. That review sits on the face of the agreement rather than buried inside it. If something is not working, a conversation usually fixes it faster than a cancellation does.
