You are not locked in. The service agreement runs month to month, and either side can end it with 30 days' notice in writing. There is no penalty, no term to buy out and nothing to cancel your way out of.

It is a written agreement and a separate document from any construction contract you may have with us. It states the scope of a visit, the cadence, your service day, the price, what is included, what is not, the notice period, and that the price is reviewed once a year. Maintenance is a standing obligation rather than a job with a finish line, so it needs terms a construction contract does not have.

The annual review is printed on the face of the agreement rather than buried inside it, because a price that never changes on paper tends to change somewhere else instead, usually in how much time the property actually gets. Once a year we look at what the garden needs and tell you where the price sits for the year ahead. You are month to month either way.

If something is not working, the better first move is usually a conversation. A cancellation is often a scope problem or a cadence problem that nobody raised while it was still easy to fix.
