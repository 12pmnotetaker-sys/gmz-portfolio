Work that rain would ruin stops; work it would not carries on. Compaction, concrete, paving, grading and planting into saturated soil all fail if you force them, so those move. Fence posts, irrigation repair, pruning, cleanup and most maintenance visits go ahead in the wet.

A weather delay is not a change order and adds nothing to your price. It moves the schedule, and we tell you the same week what moved and what the new sequence looks like. What we will not do is push a weather-sensitive task through because a date was promised. A patio set on soft base looks fine for one season and then tells on you for years.
