There is no minimum job size to ask about, so call rather than talk yourself out of it. GMZ does full residential design and build work: planting, irrigation, drainage, patios, walls, fences and lighting. What decides fit is not square footage; it is whether the work suits a build crew, a service call or a maintenance stop.

Work that is too small to mobilize a build crew does not get refused, it gets routed. An afternoon of pruning, a repair, a small refresh: where our routes allow it, that becomes a one-off service call or a maintenance visit instead. You get a straight answer on the first call, including which kind of visit your job actually is.

The one thing we will not do is send a full crew to do an hour of work and price it like a project. Different kinds of job, handled and priced differently, is what keeps all of them fair.
