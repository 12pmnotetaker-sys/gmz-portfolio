Because the earlier number was a planning range built from a walk-through and a concept, and this one is built from a measured site and materials you have chosen. That is the entire difference, and it is why this number can be committed to and the first one could not.

Three things move it, and we name which ones moved yours: the site gets measured, so quantities stop being read off photographs; materials get chosen; and the scope settles. Any of them can move a number in either direction, and a measured price sometimes comes in under the range. If it lands outside, we tell you before you open the document.
