You will not kill everything. Before we leave, the controller is set with a schedule programmed for your garden and the season, and we walk you through the basics: how to run a zone manually, how to pause for rain, and what not to touch.

Where your design agreement includes irrigation documents, you also receive the zone map and the programmed settings in writing, so any gardener or future contractor can see how the system is meant to run. Seasonal reprogramming after handover is billed by the hour, and a maintenance account includes a monthly zone-by-zone check. If the controller confuses you six months in, call.
