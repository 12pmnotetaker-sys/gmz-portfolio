Mostly you do not have to run it: we program it before we hand it over and we walk you through it on site, so you are not working it out alone. The short version is: leave the schedule we set. It is built for your zones, your soil and the season you took the garden over in.

A few things worth knowing. Zones water early in the morning, before the heat and the wind. Drip zones in the beds run long and only a couple of times a week, because they put water down slowly. Lawn and spray zones run shorter and more often. If you have a smart Wi-Fi controller, it adjusts itself with the weather, so a stretch of rain or heat does not need you to touch anything. Use the manual or single zone function when you want to run one zone and see where the water is actually going.

Change the schedule twice a year rather than constantly: down in the fall, up in late spring. If you are not sure, call before you change it. A controller that somebody reset is a common reason a new garden dies, and it is the easiest one to avoid.
