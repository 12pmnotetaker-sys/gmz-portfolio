Both are listed in writing in the bid, before you sign anything. Included is every item of work we have priced: materials and their level, labor, equipment, haul-off and the daily site services. Excluded is named just as plainly, item by item. The usual ones are permit fees, arborist reports, work inside the house, utility upgrades, and rock or buried concrete that could not be seen from the surface.

A named exclusion is more honest than a padded number, because a padded number charges you for things that may never happen. Read that page first. It is the shortest in the document and it is where nearly every contractor dispute actually lives.
