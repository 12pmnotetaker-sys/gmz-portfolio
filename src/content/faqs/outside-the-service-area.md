Usually yes, up to about forty miles from Redwood City. Past that the answer is no, for either kind of work, and you will get it on the first call rather than after somebody has made the drive. Outside the towns we list there is a $100 travel charge on top of the visit fee, because that first trip is one round trip for one conversation rather than driving spread across a full day of work.

Maintenance is stricter than the mileage suggests, and the reason is the route rather than the money. Our crews run fixed service days with stops grouped together, so a property away from an existing route means a truck leaving the route and coming back to it. For regular upkeep the honest answer is sometimes no well inside forty miles, and we would rather say so than become the company that keeps arriving late.
