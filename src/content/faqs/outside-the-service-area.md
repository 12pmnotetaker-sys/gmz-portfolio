Ask, because the answer depends on how far out you are and on which kind of work you want.

Inside the towns we list, the first visit is free and there is no travel charge. That does not change. Outside them, the first trip is the part that costs, and it is worth being straight about why. On a build project a little extra distance is usually workable, because the crew parks once in the morning and works the same ground all day, so the driving is spread across a full day of work. The visit to come and look at your property is not spread across anything: it is one round trip for one conversation. Past the towns we list, that visit carries a travel charge. You hear what it is on the call, before you agree to anything, and it is paid up front rather than settled on your doorstep.

Maintenance is stricter, and the reason is the route rather than the money. Maintenance runs on fixed service days, with stops grouped so a truck makes one trip out and works its way along. A property well away from any existing route means a truck leaving the route and coming back to it, which costs considerably more than the extra miles suggest. So for regular upkeep the honest answer is sometimes no, and you will get it early rather than after we have taken the account and become the company that keeps arriving late.

There is also a distance past which the answer is no for either kind of work. If you are near the list, ask, and you will get a straight answer on the first call rather than after somebody has made the drive.
