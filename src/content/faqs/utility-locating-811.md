Yes, every job, and no, you do not arrange it. GMZ places the locate request before any digging on your property. It is free, it is required in California, and the marks have to be on the ground before a shovel goes in, so it goes in ahead of the first day rather than being called in that morning.

One limit is worth knowing. The service marks public utilities only. It does not mark private lines, and Peninsula properties are full of them: the run out to a detached garage, pool equipment, landscape lighting, a well, a septic line. Tell us anything you know about or suspect, even vaguely, and we hand-dig wherever one is likely.
