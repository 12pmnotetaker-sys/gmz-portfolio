Yes, every job, and no, you do not arrange it. GMZ places the utility locate request before any digging on your property. It is free, it is required in California, and the marks have to be on the ground before a shovel goes in. The request carries a required notice period, so it goes in ahead of the first day of work rather than being called in that morning.

One limit is worth knowing. The locate service marks public utilities in the work area by color: gas, electric, water, communications. It does not mark private lines, and Peninsula properties are full of them: the run out to a detached garage or shed, pool equipment, landscape lighting, a well, a septic line. Those sit on your side of the meter and no agency holds a record of them. Tell us anything you know about or suspect, even vaguely, and we hand-dig wherever a private line is likely.

Skipping the call is how a routine Tuesday becomes an incident: a struck gas line, an evacuated block, a neighborhood without power, and a homeowner in the middle of a conversation they did not sign up for. There is no version of this job we do faster by not doing it.
