Yes, and we offer it at handover on every project we build. The first year is when a new garden is most easily lost: irrigation needs adjusting as roots go down, the first prunings set the shape for years, and the watering schedule that was right in May is wrong by September. None of that is difficult, but it has to actually happen.

It is a separate engagement rather than a continuation of the build. Your construction contract ends when the work is accepted. Maintenance begins after that under its own service agreement, with its own schedule and its own terms, because a recurring service and a fixed-price construction job carry different obligations on both sides.

We put the maintenance price in front of you at handover so you can decide with the number in hand instead of being asked in the abstract. No is a normal answer and it changes nothing about the build.
