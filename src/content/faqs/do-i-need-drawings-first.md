No. Most people call with photos and a rough idea, and that is enough to start. If you already have drawings from an architect or a landscape designer, GMZ can build to them; bring them to the visit and you will get an honest read on whether they are buildable as drawn and what is missing. If you have nothing, GMZ designs as well as builds.

What is needed before a firm price is a measured site and decided materials, and that is exactly what the design step produces. Nobody can price a yard accurately from photographs, and a contractor who says otherwise is guessing with your money.
