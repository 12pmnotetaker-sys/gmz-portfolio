We stop, photograph it, show it to you, price the fix and get your signature before we carry on. You will not learn about it from the final invoice.

What actually turns up in Peninsula yards: an old slab or footing under the lawn, rock, an abandoned irrigation line, a septic remnant, roots at a size where an arborist ought to look, a drain that goes nowhere, and utility lines that are not where any map says. None of it is visible from the surface, which is why it sits on the exclusions page instead of inside your price. Where we have real doubt, we suggest a test hole before you sign.
