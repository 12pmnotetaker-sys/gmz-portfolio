We stop, photograph it, show it to you, price the fix and get your signature before we carry on. You will not learn about it from the final invoice.

What actually turns up in Peninsula yards: an old slab or footing under the lawn, rock, an abandoned irrigation line, a septic remnant, tree roots at a size where an arborist ought to look at them, a drain that goes nowhere, and utility lines that are not where any map says. None of it is visible from the surface, which is precisely why it sits on the exclusions page instead of inside your price. If we padded every bid for things that are usually not there, every client would be paying for the unlucky ones.

We cut the odds where we can. We call the utility locate in before any digging, we assess the drainage rather than assume it, and where we have real doubt about what is under a surface we say so at bid time and suggest digging a test hole before you sign. A test hole costs a fraction of what the same discovery costs in week two with a crew standing on it.
