Say so, and tell us the number you had in mind. That is a normal conversation and it is far better to have it now than after a design has been drawn to a scope you cannot fund. A planning budget that lands above your expectation is doing its job: it found the gap while closing it is still cheap.

There are three usual ways to close it. The first is scope: take something out or make it smaller. A smaller paved area, fewer built structures, planting instead of a hard surface. The second is material choice, which is the biggest single lever on most projects, because the same design in a different surface material is a different price. The third is phasing the work over time, which we cover separately.

What we will not do is quietly thin the work to hit a number. Cutting base depth, drainage or soil preparation makes a job cheaper on paper and more expensive in a few years, and you are the one who pays that second bill. If a scope genuinely cannot be built properly for your budget, we would rather tell you than take the job.
