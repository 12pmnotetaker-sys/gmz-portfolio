Say so, and tell us the number you had in mind. A planning budget that lands above your expectation is doing its job: it found the gap while closing it is still cheap.

There are three usual ways to close it. Take scope out or make it smaller. Change the material level, which is the biggest single lever on most projects. Or phase the work over time. What we will not do is quietly thin the work to hit a number. Cutting base depth, drainage or soil preparation makes a job cheaper on paper and more expensive in a few years, and you are the one who pays that second bill.
