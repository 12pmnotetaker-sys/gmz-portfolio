A measured plan set for your property: a site plan drawn to scale from real measurements, the layout of paving, walls, steps and beds, a planting plan, irrigation and drainage resolved, and materials specified closely enough to price and build from. You also get the firm construction number that comes out of those plans, which a concept sketch cannot produce.

On ownership, the position we recommend and expect to put in writing is that you receive the drawings and may use them for your property, and GMZ retains the copyright in the drawings themselves. That language is being finalized, so ask to see the clause before you sign.
