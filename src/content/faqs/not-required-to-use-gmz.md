No. The garden is yours, and nothing in our agreements ties you to us for its care. You receive the deliverables scoped in your design agreement, which for most projects includes the plans and planting information a good gardener would need, and we will gladly walk your own gardener through the irrigation at handover.

We will make you an offer, because we think the crew that built a garden is the best one to establish it, and because plants are not guaranteed once their care is out of our hands. But it is an offer, not a condition, and your project price does not depend on your answer.

Whoever maintains it, give them the plant list and the irrigation settings, and keep the first year's watering honest. Most gardens that fail early fail in the establishment year, whoever is holding the hose.
