No. Hire whoever you like, keep the gardener you already have, or look after it yourself. We will not make it awkward.

What we hand over is built to work for whoever ends up caring for the garden: a copy of the plan, the plant list, the irrigation zone map with the controller settings we programmed, and a care guide covering what needs doing and roughly when. Give that to your gardener on the first day and they start well ahead of where most people start.

One thing we would rather state than bury: our position on plants is different depending on who is doing the watering. That has its own answer, and the reason is about control rather than pressure.
