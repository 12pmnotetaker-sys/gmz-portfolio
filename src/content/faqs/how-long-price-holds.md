Until the date printed on the face of the bid, which we normally set at thirty days from the day we issue it. After that we re-check material prices before we honor the number.

Thirty days is not an arbitrary deadline. Material prices in this trade move on their own schedule, stone, lumber, concrete and plant stock especially, and the prices sitting behind your number carry expiry dates of their own. A month is long enough for you to think it over and short enough that we are not committing to a price nobody can still buy. If you go past the date, tell us rather than assuming the number is dead.
