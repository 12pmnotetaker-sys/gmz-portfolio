GMZ's own crew does the work under our supervision, and no, you do not need to be home once we have what we need to get in and get water. Where a trade requires its own license, electrical work being the usual one, that portion is done by a licensed subcontractor working under our contract, and we tell you in advance which parts those are and when they are coming.

What we need from you comes up at the pre-construction meeting, before the first truck arrives: gate code or key, where the water and power are, where we can park and stage material, hours that work for you and your neighbors, anything we should know about pets and children, and one person on your side who can answer a question by phone during the day. That last one matters more than being present. A decision that sits over a weekend can hold the schedule up for a day.

There are three moments worth being there for. Layout on the first day, when the shapes get marked on the ground and are cheap to move. The irrigation and controller handover, which takes a few minutes in person and a long email otherwise. And the walkthrough at the end. Everything in between runs perfectly well on a weekly update and photographs.
