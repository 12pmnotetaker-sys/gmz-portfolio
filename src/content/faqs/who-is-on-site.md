GMZ's own crew, working under our supervision, and no, you do not need to be home once we can get in and reach water. Where a trade requires its own license, electrical being the usual one, that portion goes to a licensed subcontractor under our contract, and we tell you which parts and when.

What we need is settled at the pre-construction meeting: access, water and power, where to stage material, workable hours, anything about pets and children, and one person who can answer a question by phone. Three moments are worth being there for: layout on day one, the irrigation handover, and the final walkthrough.
