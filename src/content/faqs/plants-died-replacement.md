Not automatically, and we would rather say that up front than surprise you later. GMZ does not guarantee plants after installation. The reason is watering: once the garden is yours, we do not control how much water it gets or when it gets it, and a plant's first year is decided almost entirely by that.

What we do stand behind is our part of it: sound nursery stock, correct planting depth, prepared soil, and irrigation that was working when we handed it over. If a plant fails because of how it was planted, or because a zone was never actually running, that one is ours and we replace it. Call us and we will come and look.

The exception is when GMZ holds your maintenance account. Then the watering and the aftercare are ours, we are in the garden every week or every other week, and a plant that fails under our care is ours to make right. That is the honest reason the two things are connected, not a sales pitch.
