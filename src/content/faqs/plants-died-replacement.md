Not automatically, and we would rather say that up front than surprise you later. GMZ does not guarantee plants after installation, because once the garden is yours we do not control how much water it gets or when, and a plant's first year is decided almost entirely by that.

What we stand behind is our part of it: sound nursery stock, correct planting depth, prepared soil, and irrigation that was working when we handed it over. If a plant fails because of how it was planted, or because a zone was never actually running, that one is ours. The exception is when GMZ holds your maintenance account, where the aftercare is ours and so is the plant.
