Yes, and which one depends on what the job needs. Most small work is one person for a couple of hours and starts at $200: a sprinkler that will not come on, a controller nobody can program, a valve, a few shrubs. Work that genuinely needs two people and a truck starts at $395, because it involves weight, a machine or hauling something away. You are told which yours is and why on the call, and it is paid when you book.

Two things make a real difference to what you get for it. If several small items have been piling up, put them on one visit, because one trip covering five things is far better value than five trips covering one each. And a service call is not a consultation: a consultation buys an hour and an opinion with nothing done to the garden, while a service call is somebody arriving with tools and leaving the job finished. If you want the thing fixed rather than assessed, ask for the service call.
