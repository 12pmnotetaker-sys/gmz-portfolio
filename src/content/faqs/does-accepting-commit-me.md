Nothing. Acknowledging the planning budget is not a contract to build and it does not obligate you to spend anything. You are confirming that you have seen the scope we described, that you understand the number is a planning range rather than a fixed price, and that you would like to keep going.

It exists to prevent one specific problem, which is two people remembering the scope differently three months later. Everything we priced is listed, and everything we did not price is listed as well. That second list is the one worth reading slowly. The design agreement is the next real commitment, and the construction contract is separate again.
