Design is priced as a percentage of the construction price, and the band is 10 to 15 percent. It is the same band printed on the planning budget, so you can work out roughly what design will run as soon as you have a construction range.

Where a project lands inside the band depends on how much design there is to do: how many areas are in scope, how much grading, drainage and irrigation has to be resolved, and whether anything needs outside review. The concept and the planning budget come first as a short paid phase priced on its own, because the design fee needs a construction range to apply to.
