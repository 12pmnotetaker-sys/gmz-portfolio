Wide, and we would rather be plain about that than pretend otherwise. At the concept stage the two things that decide the final number have not happened yet: nobody has measured the site, and you have not chosen the materials. Doing both properly is what closes the gap.

It narrows in steps. When we measure. When you pick a surface material and a plant palette. When we know whether the existing surface comes out and where the debris goes. Once there is a measured plan and selected materials, the number is tight enough to commit to. The loose parts are usually demolition, drainage and anything underground.
