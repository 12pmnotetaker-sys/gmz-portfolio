Hardscape is the part of a garden that has to be right the first time. Almost
everything that goes wrong with a patio goes wrong below it: a base that was not
compacted in lifts, a subgrade that holds water, a wall built without drainage
behind it.

We build the base to what the soil and the load call for, resolve water before
the surface goes down, and detail edges so they hold their line.
