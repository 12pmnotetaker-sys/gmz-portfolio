Design comes first because it is cheaper to move a line on paper than a wall in
the ground. A plan resolves the things that are expensive to change later: where
water goes, how the grade works, how you move through the space, and what will
still be the right size in ten years.

The design is a paid step, priced as a percentage of construction, and it
produces drawings a contractor can build from and an estimator can price.
