Two halves of the same problem. Irrigation decides whether a planting plan
survives its first summer; drainage decides whether the hardscape survives its
first winter.

Zones are split by what the plants need rather than by which valve was closest,
because a bed of shrubs and a lawn panel on the same zone means one of them is
always wrong. Drainage is run to daylight wherever the grade allows it, and
where it does not, to a sump that can actually be serviced.
