Maintenance is a separate service line from design and build, with its own
agreement, its own schedule and its own terms. It is a standing obligation
rather than a project with an end date, and it is quoted that way.

A maintenance account means the same crew sees the same garden on the same day
every week. That regularity is the product: it is what turns problems into
small ones.
